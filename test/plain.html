Premier Art Auction Returns for Its 30th Year

Regional collectors eager to acquire a significant piece of historical artwork will have plenty of opportunities at this year's Annual Melbourne Art Auction. Celebrating its 30th year, organizers of this year's event have gathered an extraordinary collection of artwork that includes pieces by regional residents Chloe Delgado, Omar Berg, and Marcus Petrov.

The three painters, who were highly productive throughout their careers, achieved fame in their late 40s when they lived together in an apartment in Madrid and immersed themselves into the local art community. However, Australian fans most love the group's earlier paintings of scenes from around the southeastern coast.

Liam Fletcher, who is curating this year's auction, secured 20 pieces by the artists and expects them to bring in over $2 million in total. Mr. Fletcher said the highest estimate belongs to a painting by Ms. Delgado.
